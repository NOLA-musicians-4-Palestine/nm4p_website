About the parts

Most arrangements are split into three parts: high, mid, and low.

High and mid share the melody and counter-melody. Usually the high carries the main melody and the mid plays a counter-melody, but many arrangements trade these off — the mid takes the melody while the high takes the counter-melody / accompaniment. Whichever way, high generally sits in a higher register than mid. (These aren't “harmonies” in the Western sense: Arabic music leans on melody and counter-melody rather than stacked chords, though a little harmony shows up here and there.)

Low parts are the bass line.

Pick your part based on your instrument’s range and whether you’d rather live a bit more on the melody side or the accompaniment side — knowing you’ll do some of both.
